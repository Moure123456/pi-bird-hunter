# Pi Bird Hunter Game 🦆🐕

A web-based interactive bird hunting game designed for the Pi Network browser, featuring immersive environments, dynamic day/night cycles, a loyal hunting dog companion, and an in-game progression and shop system.

## Key Features:
- **Character & Visuals:** The hunter wears a bright orange t-shirt and black shorts, wielding a hunting rifle with smooth actions (aiming, shooting, and reloading).
- **Loyal Dog Companion:** A dog stands by the hunter, eagerly running to catch shot birds in its mouth and bringing them back to the basket.
- **Dynamic Day & Night Cycle:** 
  - *Daytime:* Clear blue skies, swaying trees, and ambient bird sounds.
  - *Nighttime:* Enhanced lighting featuring a rifle-mounted flashlight that illuminates targets as you aim.
- **Economy & Progression:** Every successful hunt rewards the player with 5 coins. Reaching 50 coins completes the level and unlocks the shop.
- **Weapon Shop:** Customize and upgrade your hunting rifles with different colors and styles.
- **Mobile-Friendly Controls:** Features a bottom-right control panel including a directional joystick, "Shoot" button, and a "Zoom" button for precision aiming.
